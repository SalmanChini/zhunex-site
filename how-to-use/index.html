Zhunex Coffee Stain Remover

Steam wand cleaning

These steps are for Coffee Stain Remover only. Other Zhunex products follow the directions on their own labels.

  1. 1

    Prepare the solution

    Add 100 ml of Zhunex. Mix with 400 ml of water.

  2. 2

    Clean the steam wand

    Open the steam valve for 3 seconds and let the solution flow through the wand.

  3. 3

    Rinse with clean water

    Repeat the process with 500 ml of clean water.

  4. 4

    Finish and dry

    Wipe the steam wand with a clean, damp cloth.

Do not use this solution for espresso machine backflush.

Everyday cleaning

  1. 1

    Add a small amount of solution.

  2. 2

    Clean with a brush, or run it through the cycle.

  3. 3

    Rinse thoroughly.

Important notes

  • Use only as directed.
  • Keep out of reach of children.
  • Avoid contact with eyes and skin.
  • Store in a cool, dry place.
  • Shake well before use.
